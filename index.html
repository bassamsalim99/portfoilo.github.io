About

01

For two and a half years at Sopra Banking Software I worked where the money actually moves: Java APIs behind retail and corporate banking platforms. My job was to break them on purpose — SQL injection, broken authentication, sloppy session handling — and then sit with the developers who owned the code until the fix was real and in production.

That work spanned engineering teams in the US, France, Spain and Brazil, so most of it came down to writing findings that a developer six time zones away could act on without a follow-up call.

I've since finished an MS in Digital Forensics and Cybersecurity at the University at Albany with a 4.0, deepening the detection side: SIEM monitoring, traffic analysis, and machine learning applied to noisy SOC data.

A vulnerability report that nobody fixes is just a document. I measure the work by what gets closed.
